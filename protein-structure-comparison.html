RMSD Interpretation Guide
< 2.0 Å
Excellent
Structures are nearly identical. Both models agree on the folding topology and secondary structure arrangements.
2.0 – 4.0 Å
Good
Some local differences. Core fold is conserved but loop regions or side-chain orientations may differ.
> 4.0 Å
Significant Divergence
Notable structural differences. May indicate different folding predictions or flexibility in disordered regions.
